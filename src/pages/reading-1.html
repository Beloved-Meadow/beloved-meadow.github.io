Meet Jesus · Reading 1 of 12

What is God like?

  • John 14:8–9
  • About 3 minutes aloud
  • For children of every age

Read this with a grown-up, or read it yourself. When you see a pause, stop and think for a moment.

A moment you might knowHearing about someone isn’t the same as seeing them

Imagine someone says to you, “There’s a hedgehog in the garden!” Would you be happy just to hear about it? Or would you run to the window, because you want to see it for yourself?

Lots of people feel like that about God. We hear about God at church, in songs and from grown-ups. But we can’t see him. So we wonder: what is God really like?

Pause. What do you imagine when you think about God?

From the BiblePhilip’s question

Retelling

It was the night before Jesus died. He was eating supper with his closest friends and talking to them about his Father, God. One of those friends was called Philip. He had been with Jesus for about three years. Then Philip asked the question he really wanted answered.

Bible text

8Philip said to him, “Lord, show us the Father, and that will be enough for us.”

9Jesus said to him, “Have I been with you such a long time, and do you not know me, Philip? He who has seen me has seen the Father. How do you say, ‘Show us the Father?’”

John 14:8–9, World English Bible

Wonder togetherLook at Jesus

Jesus said, “He who has seen me has seen the Father.” What do you think he meant?

Take your time before anyone explains.

Jesus was telling Philip that he didn’t need to look anywhere else. If you want to know what God is like, look at Jesus. So let’s look at some of the things Philip had seen:

  • A man with a skin disease, someone people kept away from. Jesus touched him.
  • Children being sent away. Jesus called them back and held them.
  • Frightened friends in a boat in a storm. Jesus woke up and calmed it.
  • A man everyone grumbled about. Jesus went to his house.

That’s what God is like. Some people imagine God as far away, or grumpy, or too busy to notice them. But Jesus says: look at me. The Father is like this.

Out of all those things Jesus did, which one would you most like to have seen?

Pause. There’s no right answer.

Something to carry today

When I wonder what God is like, I can look at Jesus.

If you’d like to respond

You can choose. You might:

  1. Borrow these words:

    Father, thank you that I can see you in Jesus. Help me get to know you better.

  2. Use your own words. Tell God one thing you’d like to know about him.
  3. Be quiet for a little while and think about Jesus touching, holding, calming and visiting. God hears quiet prayers too.

Whatever you say to God is yours. You don’t have to tell anyone.

For the grown-up

What this shows about Jesus: in Jesus, we see the Father. If we want to know how God feels about people, we look at what Jesus did.

Listening well: notice how Jesus answers Philip. He sounds surprised, but he doesn’t send Philip away for asking. He points Philip back to himself. Do the same with your child’s answers. If they picture God as angry, distant or scary, don’t correct them straight away. Ask, “What makes you think that?” and listen. Then go back to the list and look at Jesus together.

If your child asks “So is Jesus God?”, you can say yes. Christians believe Jesus is God the Son. He is not the Father, but he is one with him, so seeing Jesus really is seeing what God is like (see also John 1:18 and John 10:30).

Bible references for the list: Mark 1:40–42; Mark 10:13–16; Mark 4:35–41; Luke 19:1–10.
