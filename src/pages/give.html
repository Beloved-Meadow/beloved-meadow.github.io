Help more children meet Jesus

Everything on Beloved Meadow is free. That includes the readings, the family companions and the Sunday school sessions. Your gift helps keep it that way and makes new films and materials possible.

Why we give

“We love him, because he first loved us.” 1 John 4:19, World English Bible

What your gift makes possible

  • New short films that help children see what God is like by looking at Jesus.
  • A new Bible reading every week, written carefully and checked before it goes online.
  • Free family companions and Sunday school sessions for homes and churches that can’t pay for materials.
  • A site that stays free, with no adverts, no accounts and no tracking of children.

Give a gift

You choose the amount. You’ll be taken to a secure payment page from SumUp, where you can pay with iDEAL, a card, Apple Pay or Google Pay.

Give to Beloved Meadow

Beloved Meadow never sees or stores your payment details. Payments are handled by SumUp.

Other ways to help

  • Pray for the children and families who use these readings, that they would meet Jesus.
  • Share a reading with a family, or the Sunday school session with your church.
  • Watch and share our film What Is God Like?

Thank you. Whatever you give, and whether you give at all, we’re glad you’re here.
