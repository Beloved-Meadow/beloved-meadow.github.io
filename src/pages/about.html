Why Beloved Meadow exists

We want children to get to know Jesus, grow to love him and discover that they can talk to God honestly.

Children ask real questions. What is God like? Is there room for me? Can I still be loved after I’ve done something wrong? Can I tell God I’m scared? These questions deserve real answers. The best answers are found by looking closely at what Jesus actually said and did.

So that is what we do. We go to a specific moment in the Gospels and slow down. We ask a child what they notice before we explain it. We hope every child leaves with something specific they’ve learned about Jesus, and a question they might bring up again at bedtime, in the car or at church.

What we believe

  • Jesus is God’s Son. He is fully God and fully human. He came close so that we could see what God is like.
  • Jesus died for our sins and rose again. Through his death and resurrection, anyone who trusts him can be forgiven and know God as Father, now and forever.
  • The Bible is God’s word. It’s worth reading carefully, quoting accurately and taking seriously.
  • Jesus welcomes children. They aren’t believers-in-waiting. Jesus called them to himself.
  • Prayer can be honest. Fear, doubt, grief and sorrow can all be brought to God, as Jesus showed in Gethsemane.

How we make things

  • Bible quotations come from the World English Bible, a public-domain translation. Retellings are always labelled as retellings, and we only put words in Jesus’ mouth when they come from the passage we cite.
  • We write for children of every age, in words a young child can follow and with questions an older child can keep thinking about. We write for the adults beside them too, giving them just enough guidance to listen well.
  • We handle fear, doubt, grief and wrongdoing with care, and we never use them to pressure a child.

What we promise

  • Our main content is free to read, print and share, and it always will be.
  • Children never need an account.
  • Prayer, journaling and conversation stay at home. Nothing a child says, writes or prays is collected by this website.
  • Our films are on YouTube. We link to them directly, so you always know where you are going.
