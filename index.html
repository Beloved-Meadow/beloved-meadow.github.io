Meet Jesus

Eight questions children really ask

Each reading starts with a moment your child will recognise and goes to a real Bible passage. Then it asks what Jesus’ actions show us. They can be read in any order.

  • Reading 1

    What is God like?

    Philip wants to see the Father. Jesus tells him to look at Jesus.

    John 14:8–9
  • Reading 2

    Does Jesus have room for me?

    The disciples try to send children away. Jesus calls them closer.

    Mark 10:13–16
  • Reading 3

    Can Jesus still love me when I’ve done something wrong?

    Jesus stops under Zacchaeus’s tree and calls him by name.

    Luke 19:1–10
  • Reading 4

    Can I talk to God when I’m scared?

    Jesus prays honestly in the garden on the hardest night of his life.

    Mark 14:32–36
  • Reading 5

    Can I ask Jesus for what I need?

    The crowd says “Shh!” Jesus stands still.

    Mark 10:46–52
  • Reading 6

    What if I let Jesus down?

    Peter fails three times. Jesus makes him breakfast.

    John 21:15–17
  • Reading 7

    Why did Jesus die?

    The good shepherd doesn’t run away.

    John 10:11–15
  • Reading 8

    Is Jesus really alive?

    Thomas has doubts. Jesus comes back for him.

    John 20:24–29
